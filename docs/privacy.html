Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information World Tree processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

World Tree only processes information that is necessary to operate its Discord bot, dashboard, API, and related services. Depending on the features used, this may include guild configuration, moderation case records, no-prefix privilege records, and encrypted authentication session information.

Discord Data

When users interact with World Tree or authenticate through Discord OAuth2, the project may process Discord user IDs, usernames, guild IDs, role information, moderation actions, and limited profile information provided by Discord. This information is used solely to provide the requested functionality.

Music and Activity Features

Music playback, voice features, and activity-based systems may process temporary voice, presence, and request information required to deliver those features. This information is processed only while necessary for operation and is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics unrelated to the service.

Data Storage and Security

Persistent application data is stored in MongoDB Atlas. Authentication sessions are protected using encrypted and signed cookies. World Tree does not store OAuth access tokens in browser local storage and does not intentionally expose sensitive authentication information.

Information We Do Not Collect

World Tree does not collect passwords, payment information, private Discord messages, or personal information unrelated to providing its services. User data is never sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Data Sharing

World Tree does not sell personal information. Data may be shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests, or work with trusted infrastructure providers supporting the project.

Data Retention

Operational data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide functionality, maintain moderation history, protect server security, or operate the project. Eligible data may be removed upon request where technically and operationally feasible.

Your Rights

Users and server administrators may request deletion of eligible stored data through the Data Deletion page. Certain records may be retained where required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate server administration.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect new features, operational improvements, or legal requirements. Continued use of World Tree after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions regarding privacy or data handling may be directed through the Contact or Support pages.
